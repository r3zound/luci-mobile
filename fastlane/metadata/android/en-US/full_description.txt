Take control of your OpenWrt router or CPE with RouterPilot — a free, open-source client with no ads, no tracking, and no account.

RouterPilot talks directly to your router. Your credentials never leave your phone, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

FEATURES

• Manage multiple routers and switch between them, with a fallback address for reaching a router from another network.
• One dashboard for throughput, CPU load, memory, uptime, wireless signal, and interface status.
• See every connected client with its hostname, IP, MAC and vendor, and tell wired and wireless clients apart.
• Per-client traffic history wherever your router records it.
• Configure Wi-Fi networks and radios: SSID, security, channel, width, country code.
• Scan nearby Wi-Fi networks, and connect a radio as a station.
• Reserve DHCP addresses, block a device with a firewall rule, or wake a sleeping device over the network.
• Manage port forwards, traffic rules, firewall zones, and static routes.
• Change the hostname, timezone and description, or the router password.
• Start, stop and restart services; configure SQM, adblock, UPnP and DDNS.
• Run ping, traceroute and nslookup from the router, and read its system and kernel logs with matching lines highlighted.
• An activity feed of what changed, with optional background notifications on Android.
• Reboot the router, always behind a confirmation prompt.
• Fourteen languages, plus light and dark themes that follow your system.

SAFETY

Where the router permits it, configuration changes go through OpenWrt's own apply-with-rollback protocol: if the phone cannot confirm a change in time, the router puts the previous configuration back by itself. Where the router does not grant that permission the change is committed immediately, and the app says so while it is going in rather than leaving you to rely on a safety net that is not there. Destructive actions ask first. A firewall or traffic rule the app did not create is disabled rather than deleted.

CONNECTING

HTTP or HTTPS, including routers with a self-signed certificate — the app shows you the fingerprint and asks before trusting it, so check it against your router before you accept. Remote access works if your router and firewall are configured for it; see the README.

REQUIREMENTS

An OpenWrt router with LuCI enabled, plus the rpcd-mod-luci and rpcd-mod-iwinfo packages.

PRIVACY

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs. No data is collected and none is shared. Router credentials are kept in the device's secure storage. Full policy: https://github.com/r3zound/router-pilot/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md
