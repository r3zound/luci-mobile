RouterPilot 1.0.0 — first release under this name.

A modified fork of luci-mobile by Tunap Paul (cogwheel0). Notable changes since the fork:

• Renamed from LuCI Mobile, with a new app icon and a new application id.
• Translations completed and corrected for all 14 languages.
• Wired and wireless clients are now distinguished reliably.
• Log viewer highlights matching lines and lets you jump between older and newer entries.
• Device page simplified: fewer cards, same information.
• Better compatibility across OpenWrt distributions.

Firewall and traffic rules created by earlier builds are still recognised and can be removed.
